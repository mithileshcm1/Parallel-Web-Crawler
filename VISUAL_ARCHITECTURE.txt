Speedup
  10x │                                           ....
      │                                      .....
   8x │                                 .....
      │                            .....
   6x │                      ......         ← Diminishing Returns
      │                 .....
   4x │            .....                    ← Sweet Spot (8-12 threads)
      │       .....
   2x │   ....
      │...
   1x │_____________________________________________________
      0   4   8   12  16  20  24  28  32  36  40  Threads

Theory:  Speedup = 1 / ((1 - 0.95) + 0.95/N)
Reality: Slightly lower due to overhead

Optimal Range: 8-16 threads for most web crawling scenarios
